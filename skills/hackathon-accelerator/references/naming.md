# Naming the Project

A judge sees 50 names in a day. "FloodLine", "BreathPrint", "RoadReady", "StudyBuddy", "EcoTrack" all blur together because they use the same formula: two dictionary words in CamelCase. A short, distinctive name is easier to remember in deliberation, and easier to say on stage.

## Avoid the default formula
Don't propose names that are:
- **Two plain words glued together:** FloodLine, BreathPrint, RoadReady, CarePath, MindMap, FoodBridge. (Two words can still work, but only when the pair is unexpected, like "Paper Moon". It shouldn't be a description.)
- **Built on overused parts:** -Hub, -Sense, -Mate, -Buddy, -Wise, -Guard, -Track, -Pulse, -Flow, -Link, -Verse, -Lens, Smart-, Eco-, My-, AI suffixes ("NotesAI", "Fit.ai").
- **The product category itself:** "FloodAlert", "BudgetPlanner", "ResumeBuilder".
- **Already a famous brand**, or one letter off one.

## Patterns that make better names
Generate candidates across **at least 4 different patterns**, and no more than one or two from the "two words" pattern.

| Pattern | How | Examples (invented) |
|---|---|---|
| **Suffix blend** | A root word plus a friendly suffix: -ify, -ly, -y, -o, -io, -er, -ster | Roadify, Floody, Practicely, Tempo-ly → Temply |
| **Mash-up (portmanteau)** | Merge two words so they overlap into one new word | breath + camera → Breathmera, harvest + estimate → Harvestimate, flood + odyssey → Floodyssey |
| **Truncation** | Cut a meaningful word short, sometimes swap the ending | Engen (engine), Medi, Ambi (ambulance), Lumo (luminous) |
| **Real word, new meaning** | A short everyday word or verb that suggests what the product does | Fixit, Nudge, Tally, Ferry, Kettle, Hush |
| **Foreign or old word** | A short word from another language or an old term that fits (check what it means) | Vela (sail), Kibo (hope), Sora (sky), Halden |
| **Invented word** | Made up, 2 syllables, easy to say and spell | Zuvo, Plinko, Orra, Quill-o → Quillo |
| **Unexpected two words** (use sparingly) | Two words that create an image instead of describing the feature | Paper Moon, Night Shift, Slow Cooker |

## Checks before recommending a name
1. **Say it out loud.** Can a judge pronounce it on first sight? Is it 1–3 syllables?
2. **Spell it after hearing it.** If you'd have to spell it out every time, simplify it ("Fyxxit" fails).
3. **Search for collisions.** Use WebSearch to check that it isn't a well-known product in the same space, and look for an existing Devpost project with the same name. Note what you found. You don't need a free .com domain for a hackathon.
4. **Check meanings.** Make sure it isn't an unfortunate word in another language or slang.
5. **Fit the style.** A spec-sheet product can be "FR-7" or "Engen". A calm consumer app wants something soft like "Floody" or "Hush". A dev tool can be terse ("Postbox", "Zuvo").

## Output format
When naming, give **6–10 candidates across at least 4 patterns**, each with: the name, its pattern, a one-line reason, and the collision-check result. Recommend 2, and let the user choose. Once chosen, use the name everywhere: README, Devpost, slides, the app's header, and the repo name.
