# Devpost Submission
## Inspiration
Our teammate's street in Boulder flooded in 2013 and nobody on the block knew it was coming until the water was at the door.
## What it does
FloodWatch shows a block-by-block flood risk map built from open climate data and lets neighbors join a neighborhood alert sign-up list so they can coordinate. The insurance quote lookup in the demo uses mocked data.
## How we built it
Next.js frontend, FastAPI backend, the Open Climate Data API for precipitation forecasts, and PostGIS for spatial queries.
## Challenges we ran into
Joining coarse forecast grids to street-level elevation data fast enough to render interactively.
## What's next
SMS notifications for alert members.
